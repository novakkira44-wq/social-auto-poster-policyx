About the app

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Social Media Auto Poster is a privately operated, locally installed utility for managing media files, captions and authorized publishing to Instagram professional accounts and Facebook Pages.

Local-first: The application runs on the user's own Windows computer. This website only provides public policy and support information. It does not offer a browser-based login or upload user videos.

Important information

  • Privacy policy — what data the application uses, why and where it is stored.
  • Terms of service — conditions for use.
  • Data deletion instructions — how to remove locally stored data and disconnect social accounts.
